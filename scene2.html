I am the Rose Spirit of this garden. I have been here for a very, very long time.
This place wasn’t always like this. It used to be a garden filled with blooming blue roses.
There were many spirits like me, and people used to come here to see the roses.
But one day, people began to believe that blue roses were a bad omen.
They stopped coming here. And as time passed, they slowly forgot about us.
When a spirit is forgotten, we slowly lose the form we once had.
It’s been so long… I can barely remember my own face.
But I still remember what this garden used to look like.
Do you want to enter my MEMORY?
